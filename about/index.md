  <h2 class="subhead">What this site is for</h2>
  <p>Vietnam now has more than 90 golf courses and is one of Asia's best-value golf destinations. The catch is information: course details are scattered across operator sites, travel times are rarely stated honestly, and planning a multi-course trip takes hours of research.</p>
  <p>This guide gathers the practical facts — where courses actually are, how long it really takes to reach them, what to expect on the course — and turns them into directories and planning tools. It is deliberately focused on courses that matter for travellers: those near international airports and the country's main hotel hubs.</p>

  <h2 class="subhead">Relationship with Wingolf</h2>
  <p>Booking services referenced on this site — tee times, stay &amp; play packages, golf tours and corporate event management — are provided by <a href="{{ site.wingolf.homepage }}">Wingolf</a>, Vietnam's golf booking platform. Course details, travel times and planning guidance are researched and written independently; when you book, you book through Wingolf.</p>

  <h2 class="subhead">Sources</h2>
  <p>Course data is compiled from public sources, operator websites and official course materials, and cross-checked against booking platforms. Green fees change seasonally — treat all prices as indicative and confirm at booking.</p>
