    <h1>Phu Quoc Golf Guide</h1>
    <p>Vietnam's largest island is a stay-and-play destination: two resort courses, an international airport and some of the country's best beaches. Phu Quoc is best as a relaxed golf-and-beach break rather than a multi-round golf week.</p>

  <section class="prose">
    <h2 class="subhead">Where to stay</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Long Beach (Duong Dong):</strong> the liveliest area — markets, seafood and sunset bars, roughly 30 minutes from both courses.</li>
      <li><strong>On-resort:</strong> Eschuri sits beside the Sunset Sanato / coastal resort belt on the west coast; Vinpearl Golf anchors the Vinpearl Resort complex with its own beach.</li>
      <li><strong>North island:</strong> quieter luxury resorts near Vinpearl Safari and Grand World — combine a round with the cable car or a day at the theme parks.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="subhead">Suggested itinerary — 3 days / 2 rounds</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Day 1 — Arrival:</strong> land at PQC, settle into your resort, sunset at Long Beach.</li>
      <li><strong>Day 2 — Golf + island:</strong> morning round at Eschuri Vung Bau, afternoon beach or island hopping.</li>
      <li><strong>Day 3 — Second round or departure:</strong> Vinpearl Golf in the morning, or a lazy day before your flight.</li>
    </ol>

    <h2 class="subhead">Best season</h2>
    <p>November to March is the sweet spot — dry, sunny and low humidity. April is hot; May to October brings monsoon rain and the occasional storm. Note that Phu Quoc has only two courses, so on a longer trip plan one round per day and spend the rest on the beach.</p>
