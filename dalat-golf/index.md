  <section class="prose">
    <h2 class="subhead">Dalat Palace — the must-play</h2>
    <p>Laid out in 1923 as a mountain retreat for Vietnam's royal court, Dalat Palace is one of Southeast Asia's oldest courses and the most atmospheric round in the country. Play it early in the morning — the fairways are often misty and the 7,000-yard parkland layout plays cool and firm. It's a walking-friendly design that rewards straight, low shots through the pine-framed corridors.</p>

    <h2 class="subhead">Suggested itinerary — 3 days / 2 rounds</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Day 1 — Arrival:</strong> fly into Lien Khuong, settle in, evening walk around Xuan Huong Lake.</li>
      <li><strong>Day 2 — The classic:</strong> morning round at Dalat Palace, afternoon at Tuyen Lam Lake or the city's cafés.</li>
      <li><strong>Day 3 — The modern:</strong> SAM Tuyen Lam or The Dalat at 1200 before flying out.</li>
    </ol>

    <h2 class="subhead">Best season</h2>
    <p>Da Lat is cool all year — 15–24°C — which is exactly why you come. December to March is the driest stretch with crisp mornings; May to October brings afternoon showers that usually clear quickly. Pack a light jacket; layers are the norm at 1,500 metres.</p>
