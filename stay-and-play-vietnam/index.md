    <h2 class="subhead">What is Stay &amp; Play?</h2>
    <p>Stay &amp; Play bundles golf tee times with resort or hotel accommodation into a single package — typically a set number of nights plus one or more rounds. It turns a booking puzzle (hotel here, golf there, transfers in between) into one price, one booking and one itinerary.</p>

    <h2 class="subhead">What a package usually includes</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Green fee</strong> — 1–3 rounds depending on package length, usually on the resort's own course or a partner course nearby.</li>
      <li><strong>Accommodation</strong> — hotel or resort room, most often with breakfast.</li>
      <li><strong>Caddie</strong> — standard on nearly every course in Vietnam; a caddie walker is expected and tips run roughly 300,000–500,000 VND per round.</li>
      <li><strong>Buggy</strong> — shared electric cart, included or added at a set rate.</li>
      <li><strong>Transfers</strong> — airport pick-up/drop-off and hotel-to-course transfers, depending on the operator.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Confirm exactly which of these are inside the price — the words "stay &amp; play" cover a wide range, and the caddie fee and buggy are the two items that most often appear as extras.</p>

    <h2 class="subhead">Who should choose Stay &amp; Play?</h2>
    <p>It suits three kinds of traveller:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>First-time visitors</strong> who want one booking and no logistics surprises.</li>
      <li><strong>Groups and families</strong> mixing golf with resort time — pool, beach and a round in the same day.</li>
      <li><strong>Anyone staying at a golf resort anyway</strong> — if you're sleeping on-site, the package almost always beats paying per round.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="subhead">3D2N vs 4D3N — what's the difference?</h2>
    <p>The practical difference is rounds, not nights:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>3D2N</strong> — 2 nights, 1–2 rounds. Ideal for a long weekend or a first taste of a destination.</li>
      <li><strong>4D3N</strong> — 3 nights, 2–3 rounds. The sweet spot for a real golf holiday, especially somewhere like Da Nang where you can play different course styles each day.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>For a full golf week, stack consecutive packages or ask for a custom quote — most operators will build a longer itinerary from the same components.</p>

    <h2 class="subhead">Checklist before you book</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Are tee times confirmed at booking, or subject to availability?</li>
      <li>Which course(s) exactly — and how far from the hotel?</li>
      <li>Is the caddie fee and buggy in the price?</li>
      <li>Are airport transfers included, and are they private?</li>
      <li>Can you extend or change the number of rounds?</li>
      <li>What happens if weather cancels a round?</li>
    </ul>
