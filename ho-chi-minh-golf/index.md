    <h2 class="subhead">The highlights</h2>
    <h3>Tan Son Nhat — the time-saver</h3>
    <p>Squeezed next to the runway of Vietnam's busiest airport. At 36 holes it's the most practical option in the country: you can land, play, and fly out again in a day. Expect constant aircraft activity overhead — part of the charm for aviation fans.</p>
    <h3>Vietnam Golf &amp; Country Club — the classic</h3>
    <p>The first 36-hole golf club in Vietnam, designed by Lee Trevino and Chen King Shih. Mature parkland, a strong membership scene and consistent conditioning make it the default choice for business golf and expat groups.</p>
    <h3>Long Thanh — the weekend favourite</h3>
    <p>A Ron Fream valley-and-lake layout with the city's most generous fairways. 45–60 minutes out on the new expressway, it's the go-to for a full, relaxed day of golf.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="prose">
    <h2 class="subhead">Planning tips</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Best season:</strong> November to April is dry and pleasant. May–October is hot and wet — play early and expect afternoon storms.</li>
      <li><strong>Business golf:</strong> Tan Son Nhat and VGCC are the closest to the city and the most reliable for a half day.</li>
      <li><strong>Weekend escape:</strong> drive to The Bluffs at Ho Tram for an ocean-links round — Greg Norman's design was named World's Best New Course in 2015 — then stay beachfront overnight.</li>
    </ul>
