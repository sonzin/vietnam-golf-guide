  <section class="prose">
    <h2 class="subhead">Where to stay</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>My Khe beachfront (Da Nang city):</strong> best all-round base — 15–25 minutes to most courses and 15 minutes to the airport.</li>
      <li><strong>Hoi An:</strong> quieter and more atmospheric, 45–60 minutes from the courses but on the doorstep of Hoiana Shores and Montgomerie.</li>
      <li><strong>On-resort:</strong> Laguna Lang Co and Hoiana are stay-and-play destinations in their own right, with hotel, beach and multiple golf options.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="subhead">How many days</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>2 rounds / 3 days:</strong> a relaxed short break — one coastal links day (Montgomerie + BRG Legend are 2 minutes apart for a 36-hole day) and one mountain day at Ba Na Hills.</li>
      <li><strong>4–5 rounds / 5 days:</strong> add Hoiana Shores and a trip to Laguna Lang Co, with a free day for Hoi An or Ba Na Hills mountain park.</li>
      <li><strong>7+ days:</strong> extend north to Hue or fly south to Nha Trang for links golf at KN Golf Links Cam Ranh.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="subhead">Suggested 3-day itinerary</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Day 1 — Arrival:</strong> land at DAD, settle in at My Khe, optional evening putting practice or sunset at Hoi An.</li>
      <li><strong>Day 2 — Coastal links:</strong> morning at Montgomerie Links, afternoon at BRG Legend Da Nang (2-minute transfer).</li>
      <li><strong>Day 3 — Mountain day:</strong> Ba Na Hills Golf Club in the morning, then the cable car up to Ba Na Hills mountain park — or a second round.</li>
    </ol>

    <h2 class="subhead">Best season</h2>
    <p>February to September brings dry, sunny weather. Rain concentrates in October–December; January can be cool and overcast. Book the popular coastal links on weekday mornings in peak season (March–August).</p>
