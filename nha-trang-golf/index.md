  <section class="prose">
    <h2 class="subhead">Where to stay</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Nha Trang beachfront:</strong> the classic base — hotels along Tran Phu beach, 25–35 minutes from the courses and 40 minutes from the airport.</li>
      <li><strong>Cam Ranh peninsula:</strong> quieter and closer to KN Golf Links; several new luxury resorts (Alma, The Anam) sit minutes from the course.</li>
      <li><strong>On-resort:</strong> Diamond Bay Golf &amp; Villas and Vinpearl both run stay-and-play packages with 18 holes included.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="subhead">Suggested itinerary — 4 days / 3 rounds</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Day 1 — Arrival:</strong> land at Cam Ranh, check in, settle on the beach.</li>
      <li><strong>Day 2 — Links day:</strong> KN Golf Links Cam Ranh (Greg Norman) in the morning; the course's ocean views are at their best early.</li>
      <li><strong>Day 3 — Challenge day:</strong> Diamond Bay Golf Club — a strong par 72 that rewards accurate play; book a caddie who knows the winds.</li>
      <li><strong>Day 4 — Island round or departure:</strong> Vinpearl Golf on the island, or an easy morning before flying out.</li>
    </ol>

    <h2 class="subhead">Best season</h2>
    <p>February to September is sunny and dry — the ideal window. October to December brings rain and cooler sea temperatures; January can be overcast. Book weekday mornings in peak season (June–August) to avoid the heat.</p>

    <div class="note">
      <strong>Combine with Phan Thiet:</strong> on a longer trip, PGA NovaWorld Phan Thiet (36 holes) is a 3-hour drive south along the coast — pair it with a beach resort stop.
    </div>
